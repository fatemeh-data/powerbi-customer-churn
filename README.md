# 📊 Customer Churn Analysis Dashboard | Power BI

Identify key drivers of churn and boost retention strategies

## 📊 Overview

Customer churn is a major challenge for subscription-based businesses.  

This Power BI dashboard helps analysts and managers:  
- Identify churn patterns  
- Understand customer behavior  
- Make data-driven retention decisions
---
## 🎯 Objectives / Goals

This dashboard aims to help businesses and analysts:  
- Analyze customer behavior trends and churn patterns  
- Detect high-risk churn segments  
- Evaluate impact of contracts, support interactions, and demographics on retention  
- Provide actionable recommendations to reduce churn

---
## 📈 Key Metrics & Key Insights

**Key Metrics – Core Dashboard Measurements**

| Metric | Value | Description |
|--------|-------|-------------|
| **Total Customers** | 6,687 | Total number of customers in the dataset |
| **Churned Customers** | 1,796 | Number of customers who churned |
| **Churn Rate** | 26.86% | Percentage of customers who churned |
| **Estimated Revenue Loss** | $1.36M | Estimated financial impact due to churn |

**Key Insights – Major Findings**

1. **Contract Type Drives Churn:** Month-to-month customers exhibit a higher churn rate, indicating lower commitment and higher risk.  
2. **Customer Behavior Matters:** Low engagement strongly correlates with churn; active usage is a leading indicator.  
3. **Customer Support Signals Risk:** Customers who churned had more support interactions, highlighting potential dissatisfaction or unresolved issues.  
4. **Churn is Multi-Dimensional:** A combination of behavioral, contractual, and service factors explains churn, not just pricing.

---
## 📝 Business Recommendations & Impact

**Recommendations:**  
- Encourage longer-term contracts to improve retention  
- Provide targeted support for high-risk segments  
- Develop loyalty programs for customers with higher churn probability  

**Impact:**  
- **Proactively identify high-risk customers** and take preventive measures  
- **Reduce churn** through targeted retention strategies  
- **Improve customer satisfaction and retention** by addressing pain points  
- **Support data-driven decision-making** for long-term business growth

## 📂 Dataset / Source

**Source:** Customer churn case study dataset (training project)  

**Description:**  
Customer-level dataset including demographics, contract details, usage behavior, customer support interactions, and financial metrics.

**Key Features:**  
- **Demographics:** Age, Gender, Customer Group  
- **Contract Information:** Contract Type, Payment Method, Account Length  
- **Usage Behavior:** Call activity, Data consumption, International usage  
- **Customer Experience:** Customer service calls, churn reasons  
- **Financial Data:** Monthly charges, Total charges, Extra fees  

**Target Variable:**  
- **Churn Label:** Yes / No

## 🖥️ Dashboard Pages

The dashboard consists of the following main pages:

1. **Executive Overview**  
   Provides high-level KPIs, overall trends, and key metrics at a glance for quick business insights.

2. **Customer Demographics & Behavior**  
   Analyzes customer profiles, age, gender, and behavioral patterns to identify trends related to churn.

3. **Contract & Pricing Drivers**  
   Examines the impact of contract types, payment methods, and pricing on customer retention and churn.

4. **Product & Usage Behavior**  
   Visualizes customer interactions with products and services, including usage frequency and engagement patterns.

5. **Customer Support & Engagement**  
   Tracks customer support interactions, engagement levels, and identifies potential churn signals.
