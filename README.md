 ## Customer Churn Analysis Dashboard | Power BI

## 📊 Business Problem
Customer churn is a critical challenge for subscription-based businesses, directly affecting revenue, growth, and long-term sustainability.

While many organizations track churn rates, they often lack visibility into the underlying drivers behind customer attrition.

---

## 🎯 Project Objective
This project aims to analyze customer data and uncover the key drivers of churn, focusing on customer behavior, contract structure, and service experience.

---

## 📌 Key Metrics
- Total Customers: 6,687  
- Churn Rate: 26.86%  
- Churned Customers: 1,796  
- Estimated Revenue Loss: $1.36M  

---

## 🔍 Key Insights

### 📉 Contract Type Drives Churn
Customers with month-to-month contracts exhibit significantly higher churn rates, indicating low commitment and higher risk.

### 📊 Customer Behavior Matters
Low engagement levels strongly correlate with churn, making usage a key leading indicator.

### ☎️ Customer Support Signals Risk
Customers who churn tend to have more support interactions, suggesting dissatisfaction or unresolved issues.

### 💡 Churn is Multi-Dimensional
Churn is driven by a combination of behavioral, operational, and contractual factors—not just pricing.

---

## 💡 Business Recommendations
Based on the analysis, the following actions are recommended:

- 🎯 Encourage long-term contracts through targeted incentives  
- 📉 Identify low-engagement users and trigger retention campaigns  
- 📞 Improve customer support resolution time and quality  
- 🔔 Implement churn prediction monitoring for early intervention  

---

## 🚀 Business Impact
This analysis enables organizations to:

- Proactively identify high-risk customers  
- Reduce churn through targeted strategies  
- Improve customer satisfaction and retention  
- Support data-driven decision-making
