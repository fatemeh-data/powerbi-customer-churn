# Customer Churn Analysis Dashboard | Power BI

---

## 📊 Overview

Customer churn is one of the most critical challenges in subscription-based businesses, directly impacting revenue and long-term growth.

This project goes beyond measuring churn and focuses on identifying the key factors that drive customer decisions.

---

## 🎯 Objective

To analyze customer data and uncover the key drivers of churn, focusing on:

* Customer behavior
* Contract structure
* Customer experience

---

## 📌 Key Metrics

* Total Customers: 6,687
* Churn Rate: 26.86%
* Churned Customers: 1,796
* Estimated Revenue Loss: $1.36M

---

## 🔍 Key Insights

### 📉 Contract Type Drives Churn

Customers with month-to-month contracts show significantly higher churn rates, indicating lower commitment and higher risk.

### 📊 Customer Behavior Matters

Low engagement strongly correlates with churn, making usage a key leading indicator.

### ☎️ Customer Support Signals Risk

Customers who churn have more support interactions, suggesting dissatisfaction or unresolved issues.

### 💡 Churn is Multi-Dimensional

Churn is driven by a combination of behavioral, contractual, and service-related factors—not just pricing.

---

## 💡 Business Recommendations

* Encourage long-term contracts through targeted incentives
* Identify low-engagement users and trigger retention campaigns
* Improve customer support resolution and experience
* Monitor churn risk for early intervention

---

## 🚀 Business Impact

This analysis helps organizations to:

* Proactively identify high-risk customers
* Reduce churn through targeted strategies
* Improve customer satisfaction and retention
* Support data-driven decision-making

## 📁 Dataset

**Source:** Customer churn case study dataset (training project)

**Description:**
Customer-level dataset including demographics, contract details, usage behavior, customer support interactions, and financial metrics.
- Key Features:
  - Demographics: Age, Gender, Customer Group  
  - Contract Information: Contract Type, Payment Method, Account Length  
  - Usage Behavior: Call activity, Data consumption, International usage  
  - Customer Experience: Customer service calls, churn reasons  
  - Financial Data: Monthly charges, total charges, extra fees  
- Target Variable: Churn Label (Yes/No)
